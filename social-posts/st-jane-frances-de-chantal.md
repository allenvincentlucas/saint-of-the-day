Widowed at 28 with four children, she spent seven years living under the roof of a bitter housekeeper her father-in-law refused to dismiss — and it still wasn't the end of her story.

Jane Frances de Chantal found a spiritual director in Francis de Sales, and at 45 co-founded the Visitation Order — built, unusually for the time, to accept women other convents turned away for age or poor health. She buried Francis, lost a son in battle, and nursed plague victims with her convent's own resources, all while founding new houses across France. She died still traveling to visit them.

Today is her feast day.
