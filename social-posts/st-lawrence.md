Ordered to hand over the Church's treasury, a young Roman deacon asked for three days — then returned with the city's poor and called them the treasure instead.

Lawrence was archdeacon under Pope Sixtus II, martyred four days after his bishop during Emperor Valerian's persecution in 258 AD. Tradition holds he was executed on a gridiron, and even joked with his executioners mid-torment — though historians think he may simply have been beheaded like the others. Either way, he became one of the most venerated martyrs in Rome, second only to Peter and Paul.

Today is his feast day.
