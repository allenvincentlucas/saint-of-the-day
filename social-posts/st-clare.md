At eighteen, she slipped out of her family's palace at night, walked to a small chapel, and let Francis of Assisi cut off her hair — then clung to the altar when her family tried to drag her home.

Clare became the first female follower of St. Francis and spent forty years fighting the Church itself for the right to own absolutely nothing. Popes worried it was impractical; she never backed down. She finally won papal approval for her rule just two days before she died — the first religious rule known to have been written by a woman.

Today is her feast day.
