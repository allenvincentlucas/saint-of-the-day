He watched Cathar preachers win people over through visible poverty — and noticed the Church's own preachers, dressed as clergy, kept losing that argument.

Dominic gave up his horse, his lodging, and his comfort, and walked from village to village debating heresy on equal terms of poverty instead. In 1216 he founded the Order of Preachers, and a year later scattered his own tiny community — just seventeen friars — across Europe, telling them grain that's stored rots, but grain that's sown bears fruit. It worked: within decades the Dominicans had produced St. Thomas Aquinas.

Today is his feast day.
